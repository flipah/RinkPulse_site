How do I see speed, distance, and shifts?
Start a RinkPulse workout, then move through the in-game pages. DOWN or swipe down/left goes to the next page on current builds. Speed and distance are on the Speed & Distance page. Shift count, average shift, longest shift, and work/rest are on the Shifts page. See the user guide for the full walkthrough.
Which Garmin watches work?
A wide set of Forerunner, fenix, Instinct, Venu, vivoactive, epix, Enduro, MARQ, D2, Descent, and Approach watches that support Connect IQ watch apps. If your watch can install third-party Connect IQ apps, it may be supported.
How does it know I'm on the bench?
Motion, speed/distance evidence when available, short heart-rate context, and gyroscope-backed confidence on supported watches. It is not perfect, especially on weird bench changes or short shifts, but the goal is no button pressing every shift.
Do I need the Android app?
No. The watch works standalone and shows you workout stats post-game. The Android app is for keeping session history, looking at HR/shift charts on a bigger screen, and editing score or personal stats after a manual entry or Garmin import. If you want to help test Android, join the Play open test and send feedback with your Garmin watch model, Android phone model, and what kind of skate you tried.
Can I track goals and score?
Yes, but skater scorekeeping is handled in RinkPulse Companion after the session instead of on the watch during play. Open the session on Android and use Edit Stats for goals, assists, plus/minus, team score, opponent score, or notes. Goalie mode still supports save and goal-against entry from the watch.
What should I update before my next skate?
Update RinkPulse on your Garmin watch from Connect IQ and update RinkPulse Companion on Android from Google Play or the open test link. Open the Android app once after updating so it can check the watch connection and prepare its local history backup. If your Android history looks wrong, do not uninstall or clear app storage before contacting support.
iPhone? Apple Watch?
Not yet. Possibly. No promise on timing. It's a side project.
How much?
Free right now. No ads. The Garmin watch app and local Android history do not require an account. Optional Android cloud saves use a RinkPulse Companion sign-in. Premium purchase is not live.
Can I send feedback?
Please. rinkpulsesupport@gmail.com. Bug reports with watch model, phone model, and what happened are gold.
