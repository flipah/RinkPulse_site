How do I see speed, distance, and shifts?
Start a RinkPulse workout, then move through the in-game pages. DOWN or swipe down/left goes to the next page on current builds. Speed and distance are on the Speed & Distance page. Shift count, average shift, longest shift, and work/rest are on the Shifts page. See the user guide for the full walkthrough.
Which Garmin watches work?
A wide set of Forerunner, fenix, Instinct, Venu, vivoactive, epix, Enduro, MARQ, D2, Descent, and Approach watches that support Connect IQ watch apps. If your watch can install third-party Connect IQ apps, it may be supported.
How does it know I'm on the bench?
Motion, speed/distance evidence when available, short heart-rate context, and gyroscope-backed confidence on supported watches. It is not perfect, especially on weird bench changes or short shifts, but the goal is no button pressing every shift.
Do I need the companion app?
No. The watch works standalone and shows you workout stats post-game. The iOS and Android companion apps are for keeping session history, looking at HR/shift charts on a bigger screen, editing score or personal stats after a manual entry or Garmin import, and optionally syncing RinkPulse history after sign-in.
Can I track goals and score?
Yes, but skater scorekeeping is handled in RinkPulse Companion after the session instead of on the watch during play. Open the session on iPhone or Android and use Edit Stats for goals, assists, plus/minus, team score, opponent score, or notes. Goalie mode still supports save and goal-against entry from the watch.
What should I update before my next skate?
Update RinkPulse on your Garmin watch from Connect IQ and update RinkPulse Companion on iPhone through TestFlight or on Android from Google Play. Open the companion app once after updating so it can check the watch connection and prepare local history.
iPhone? Apple Watch?
The iPhone companion is in TestFlight for testers. The Apple Watch is not supported right now; the watch-tracking app is still Garmin-first.
What does RinkPulse not support yet?
RinkPulse does not currently provide Apple Watch tracking, team or line-wide stats, full Garmin account history import, or a public iOS App Store release. The iPhone companion is available through TestFlight, and the Android companion is available through an open Google Play test.
How much ice time should I expect?
It depends on game length, how many lines rotate, and how evenly the bench is managed. Use the free Hockey Shift & Ice-Time Calculator for a quick estimate of ice time, shift count, bench recovery, and work/rest ratio.
How much?
Free right now. No ads. The Garmin watch app and local companion history do not require an account. Optional cloud saves use a RinkPulse Companion sign-in. Premium purchase is not live.
Can I send feedback?
Please. rinkpulsesupport@gmail.com. Bug reports with watch model, phone model, and what happened are gold.
