Data Deletion

RinkPulse account and app data requests

RinkPulse Companion is local-first. If you choose to sign in for cloud saves, your synced RinkPulse session history is connected to that RinkPulse app account. You can delete that data from inside the iOS or Android app or by contacting support.

Delete in the app

  1. Open RinkPulse Companion on iPhone or Android.
  2. Go to Profile. On iPhone, use the Cloud Saves section. On Android, go to Data & Sync.
  3. Tap Delete Cloud Account Data or Delete account data.
  4. Confirm the destructive deletion prompt.

This removes RinkPulse cloud session data for the signed-in account, deletes the app account used for cloud sync, removes that account's synced local copies, and signs you out. Local-only sessions that were never added to the cloud account remain on the device and can be deleted individually.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app, email rinkpulsesupport@gmail.com with subject RinkPulse Data Deletion Request. Include the email address you used to sign in to RinkPulse Companion. I may need to reply to verify the request before deleting cloud account data.

Email deletion request

What is deleted

  • RinkPulse cloud session records tied to your RinkPulse Companion account.
  • The RinkPulse app account used for cloud sync.
  • Local synced session copies owned by that cloud account when you use the in-app deletion action.

What is not deleted

  • Garmin Connect activities, Garmin account history, or Garmin account data.
  • Local-only RinkPulse sessions that were never added to the deleted cloud account.
  • Google Play purchase or install records controlled by Google.
  • Emails you already sent to support, unless you ask for those to be removed too.
  • Records I am required to keep for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute handling.

Timing

In-app deletion is intended to run immediately. Email deletion requests are handled as soon as practical and no later than 30 days after verification unless a legal or security exception applies.

Questions

Email rinkpulsesupport@gmail.com.
