Support

Common questions

Is there a user guide?

Yes. Start here: RinkPulse User Guide. It explains starting a workout, finding speed/distance and shifts, goalie mode, phone sync, and what to try when a session does not import.

How do I get speed, distance, and shifts?

Start a RinkPulse workout on the watch, then move through the in-game pages. DOWN or swipe down/left goes to the next page on current builds. Speed and distance are on the Speed & Distance page. Shift count and work/rest are on the Shifts page.

My session is not syncing to the phone

First update both apps: RinkPulse on the Garmin watch from Connect IQ, and RinkPulse Companion on iPhone through TestFlight or on Android from Google Play. Then open Garmin Connect Mobile and confirm the watch is connected. Open RinkPulse Companion and retry the watch backlog: on iPhone, use Profile, Select Garmin Watch if needed, then Retry Watch Backlog; on Android, use the Garmin sync card on Home. If it still does not import, open RinkPulse on the watch to the main mode-select screen and retry again in the companion app. More details are in the sync guide.

My companion history disappeared

Update RinkPulse Companion before doing anything else. Do not uninstall the app, clear app storage, switch install sources, or move between phone profiles while troubleshooting. Open the app once after updating so it can prepare local history where supported. If the app opens, go to Profile and use Export support diagnostics if available.

How do I delete cloud account data?

In RinkPulse Companion, go to Profile. On iPhone, use the Cloud Saves section; on Android, go to Data & Sync. Use the delete cloud account data action and confirm the destructive prompt. If you cannot open the app, use the data deletion page to request deletion by email.

How do I edit score or personal stats?

Skater score and stat edits are handled in RinkPulse Companion after a manual entry or Garmin import. Open the session on iPhone or Android and use Edit Stats to adjust goals, assists, plus/minus, team score, opponent score, or notes. The watch stays focused on workout tracking; goalie mode still supports save and goal-against entry from the watch.

How do I delete one session?

Open the session in RinkPulse Companion, tap Delete, then confirm. This removes the session from the phone. If cloud sync is enabled, RinkPulse also queues that session delete for your cloud history.

The watch didn't detect my shifts

Make sure the heart-rate sensor is on and the watch is snug. Shift detection uses motion, speed or distance evidence when available, short heart-rate context, and gyroscope-backed confidence on supported watches. Short shifts and unusual bench changes can be harder to classify. If you use the companion app, import the session and include the detector diagnostics from the session detail screen or the exported support diagnostics.

How do I install the watch app?

Use the Garmin Connect IQ link from the home page or search for "RinkPulse" in Connect IQ. You will need a compatible Garmin watch that supports Connect IQ watch apps.

How do I join the iOS TestFlight?

Join the iPhone companion beta using the public TestFlight link below. Open it on your iPhone and follow the steps to install TestFlight and RinkPulse Companion. No email invite is needed. If you have trouble joining, email support with your iPhone model and what you see.

How do I install the Android app?

The Android companion is available through the Google Play open test. Use the web opt-in link first, then install RinkPulse Companion from Google Play on Android. If Play says the test is unavailable, the release may still be under review, not available in your country yet, or opened from a Google account that cannot join the test.

  1. Join on the web and opt in with the Google account you use on your Android phone.
  2. Install on Android from Google Play after opting in.
