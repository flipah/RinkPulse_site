Support

It's a side project, so support is mostly me, between shifts.

Common questions

Is there a user guide?

Yes. Start here: RinkPulse User Guide. It explains starting a workout, finding speed/distance and shifts, goalie mode, Android sync, and what to try when a session does not import.

How do I get speed, distance, and shifts?

Start a RinkPulse workout on the watch, then move through the in-game pages. DOWN or swipe down/left goes to the next page on current builds. Speed and distance are on the Speed & Distance page. Shift count and work/rest are on the Shifts page.

My session is not syncing to Android

First update both apps: RinkPulse on the Garmin watch from Connect IQ, and RinkPulse Companion on Android from Google Play or the open test link. Then open Garmin Connect Mobile and confirm the watch is connected. Open RinkPulse Companion, wait for the Garmin sync card, and tap Retry. If it still does not import, open RinkPulse on the watch to the main mode-select screen and tap Retry again in the Android app. More details are in the sync guide.

My Android history disappeared

Update RinkPulse Companion before doing anything else. Do not uninstall the app, clear app storage, switch install sources, or move between phone profiles while troubleshooting. Open the Android app once after updating so it can prepare its local history backup. If the app opens, go to Profile, then Data & Sync, and use Export support diagnostics.

How do I delete cloud account data?

In RinkPulse Companion, go to Profile, then Data & Sync, tap Delete account data, then Confirm delete data. If you cannot open the app, use the data deletion page to request deletion by email.

How do I edit score or personal stats?

Skater score and stat edits are handled in RinkPulse Companion after a manual entry or Garmin import. Open the session on Android and use Edit Stats to adjust goals, assists, plus/minus, team score, opponent score, or notes. The watch stays focused on workout tracking; goalie mode still supports save and goal-against entry from the watch.

How do I delete one session?

Open the session in RinkPulse Companion, tap Delete, then confirm. This removes the session from the phone. If cloud sync is enabled, RinkPulse also queues that session delete for your cloud history.

The watch didn't detect my shifts

Make sure the heart-rate sensor is on and the watch is snug. Shift detection uses motion, speed or distance evidence when available, short heart-rate context, and gyroscope-backed confidence on supported watches. Short shifts and unusual bench changes can be harder to classify. If you use Android, import the session and include the detector diagnostics from the session detail screen or the exported support diagnostics.

How do I install the watch app?

Use the Garmin Connect IQ link from the home page or search for "RinkPulse" in Connect IQ. You will need a compatible Garmin watch that supports Connect IQ watch apps.

How do I install the Android app?

The Android companion is available through the Google Play open test. Use the Play link, opt in, then install RinkPulse Companion from Google Play. If Play says the test is unavailable, the release may still be under review, not available in your country yet, or opened from a Google account that cannot join the test.
